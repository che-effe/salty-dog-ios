# Salty Dog Mobile – FAQ & Support

## General

**Q: What is Salty Dog Mobile?**  
A: Salty Dog Mobile is an app for anglers and boaters to log catches, track trips, and view real-time weather and tide data.

**Q: Is my data private?**  
A: Yes. All your logs and data are stored locally on your device and are not shared unless you choose to export them.

---

## Logging & Tracking

**Q: How do I log a catch?**  
A: Tap the “Mark Fishing Spot” button, fill in the details, and save your catch. You can add photos, notes, and location info.

**Q: Can I edit or delete a log?**  
A: Yes. Tap on any log entry to view, edit, or delete it.

**Q: How do I view my fishing statistics?**  
A: Tap the chart icon in the top right to see your stats and patterns.

---

## Weather, Tides & Marine Data

**Q: Where does the weather and tide data come from?**  
A: The app uses Apple WeatherKit and NOAA for US marine data. International fallback is available via StormGlass (if enabled).

**Q: Why don’t I see water temperature or tide info?**  
A: These features require location access and internet connectivity. Some regions may not have available data.

**Q: How often is the data updated?**  
A: Weather and marine data refresh automatically as your location changes, or you can manually refresh by reopening the app.

---

## Permissions & Privacy

**Q: Why does the app need my location?**  
A: Location is used to log catches, track your route, and fetch local weather/tide data. You can disable location, but some features won’t work.

**Q: Does the app track me in the background?**  
A: Only if you enable background location in settings. This helps track your route while fishing or boating.

---

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Weather or tide data isn’t loading. What should I do?**
- Ensure you have an active internet connection.
- Make sure location permissions are enabled in your device settings.
- Try closing and reopening the app.
- If the issue persists, check for app updates in the App Store.

**Q: My catches or logs are missing.**
- Check if you’re logged into the correct device/user.
- Data is stored locally; if you deleted the app, logs may be lost unless exported.

**Q: The app is crashing or freezing.**
- Restart your device.
- Ensure you’re running the latest version of iOS and the app.
- If problems continue, contact support with details about your device and what you were doing when the issue occurred.
